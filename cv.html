Journal articles

  • 2026

    Circularly polarized light detection via toroidal resonance using an X-shaped chiral metasurface

    Seong Jun Kim†, Yu Geun Ki†, Hyun Jin Jung, and Soo Jin Kim

    Photonics Research 14(4), 1220–1229 (2026)

    Co-first author

    X-shaped silicon metasurfaces control the interference of magnetic toroidal dipoles to distinguish circular-polarization handedness. Simulations and experiments demonstrate selective absorption, with a nanobeam-array design enabling polarization-selective photocurrent.

    Read the paper ↗
  • 2024

    Realizing Minimally Perturbed, Nonlocal Chiral Metasurfaces for Direct Stokes Parameter Detection

    Yu Geun Ki, Byeong Je Jeon, Il Hoon Song, Seong Jun Kim, Sangtae Jeon, and Soo Jin Kim

    ACS Nano 18(9) (2024)

    First author

    Small geometric perturbations in elliptical silicon nanoposts couple local Mie resonances with high-Q nonlocal resonances. The resulting metasurfaces selectively detect polarization states within a narrow spectral band, enabling direct Stokes-parameter detection.

    Read the paper ↗
  • 2024

    Design of Metasurface-Based Photodetector with High-Quality Factor

    Yu Geun Ki, Hyeon Woo Jeon, and Soo Jin Kim

    Electronics 13, 753 (2024)

    First author

    This design study separates the resonance-forming dielectric nanoposts from the silicon absorption and charge-collection layer. Simulations show how symmetry breaking balances radiative and absorption losses for narrowband, polarization-selective near-infrared photodetection.
