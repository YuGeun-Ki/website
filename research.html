01

Polarization sensing & optical decoding

Nonlocal resonance · Photodetection

Nonlocal and chiral metasurfaces translate polarization into selective optical and electrical responses. I design simple resonator geometries and use their symmetry and coupling to extract optical information.

Published research

Identical elliptical nanoposts enable diverse polarization responses through group-cell symmetry breaking (ACS Nano, 2024). An X-shaped chiral metasurface uses toroidal resonance for filterless circularly polarized photodetection (Photonics Research, 2026; co-first author).

02

Spectral routing & near-infrared detection

RGB–NIR routing · Guided-mode resonance

I investigate metasurface architectures that separate spectral channels and enhance absorption in thin silicon detectors, with image-sensor integration as a motivating application.

Published research

A single-layer silicon nitride metasurface routes RGB and NIR light to separate pixels (Nanophotonics, 2024; co-author). A high-Q metasurface couples resonances to a silicon absorbing layer for near-infrared photodetection (Electronics, 2024; first author).

03

Device fabrication & optical characterization

Design → Fabrication → Measurement

My work spans electromagnetic modeling, fabrication-aware design, device fabrication, optical measurements, and data analysis.

Collaborative research

Additional published work covers symmetry-dependent antireflection, planar intrinsic chirality, and mechanically tunable bilayer metasurfaces for beam shaping.
